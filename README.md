### 🔥 Featured Projects

#### 🦁 [Leo: The On-Device Financial Mentor](https://github.com/raythekool/edge-skill-financial-advisor)
An empathetic AI educator for the Google AI Edge Gallery. Leo runs locally on your device, tracks your financial literacy progress, awards gamified badges, and generates daily economic news—all while strictly enforcing an ethical "No-Advice" rule. Built entirely in Vanilla JS for maximum speed and zero dependencies.

#### ⛈️ [Italian Weather Alerts (Protezione Civile)](https://github.com/raythekool/edge-skill-italian-weather-alerts)
A decentralized Edge skill that bypasses generic web searches to query the official Italian Civil Protection Open Data repository directly from your device. It provides real-time, localized hydrogeological alert maps and color-coded bulletins directly within an AI chat interface.
